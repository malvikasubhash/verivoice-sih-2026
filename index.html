LEARN

Understand the threat.

Voice cloning can make a familiar voice sound convincing enough to trigger rushed decisions.

🧬

Voice Cloning

AI systems can synthesize speech from voice samples. A familiar voice is not, by itself, proof of identity.

🎭

Impersonation

Attackers can combine a cloned voice with social engineering to pressure a victim into acting quickly.

🔐

Verify Independently

Use a second trusted channel before transferring money, sharing OTPs or revealing confidential information.

⚠️

Risk Is a Signal

An AI result should guide caution and verification. It should not be treated as absolute proof of identity.
